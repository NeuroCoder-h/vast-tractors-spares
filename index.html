ABOUT FIELD ESSENTIALS

Parts for the machinery that keeps work moving.

Field Essentials Investments (Pvt) Ltd, trading as Vast Tractors Spares, is a Harare-based supplier of tractor and TLB spare parts.

We support customers looking for parts for a range of established brands, including Fiat, Ford, Case, New Holland, Massey Ferguson and Landini.

Our goal is straightforward: help farmers, machinery owners, workshops and businesses find the parts they need to keep their equipment working.
